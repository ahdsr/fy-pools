Competitive homepage scan

Ten active, relevant alternatives to PoolWaffle, captured on 14 July 2026. This is a practical market scan of broad hosts, hockey specialists, and current World Cup entrants—not a claim that every global sports-pool site has been catalogued.

Screens are default desktop viewports. Copy and feature lists reflect the live homepages and linked public marketing pages at capture time.

RunYourPool

runyourpool.com · broad US host / Splash brand

Seasonal, social sports pools with a World Cup campaign and an optional cash-contest path.

  • Leads with a World Cup group-ranking plus knockout-bracket pool.
  • Claims 1M+ users and covers NFL, college basketball, golf, NBA, MLB, NHL, soccer, Olympics, and awards.
  • Promotes easy setup, free first week, configurable scoring, online picks, automatic deadlines, live standings, reporting, and a message board.
  • Frames cash collection and winner payouts as part of its Splash flow.

OfficePools

officepools.com · broad Canadian fantasy platform

“Fantasy competition, your way” with Canadian heritage and multiple pool types.

  • Hero emphasizes choice; navigation foregrounds fantasy pools, Pool Lab, fundraising, and resources.
  • Sports span hockey, golf, soccer, football, curling, and entertainment.
  • Explains a four-step flow: create, invite, choose a team, track progress.
  • Marketing pages list Draft, Box, Dynasty, Pick’em, Survivor, and Open pools; real-time scoring and player news.

Office Pool Junkie

officepooljunkie.com · free, format-led host

The plainspoken spreadsheet replacement: “Fun, Free and Easy Office Pools.”

  • Directly calls out paper, email, and spreadsheets as the pain point.
  • Uses a pool wizard and “up and running in no time” promise.
  • Offers bracket, confidence, margin, pick’em, power ranking, squares, survivor, and more.
  • Covers a broad North American and football/soccer league catalogue.

PoolHost

poolhost.com · free office-pool host

A direct “throw away the spreadsheet” product pitch, with a traditional sports aesthetic.

  • Leads with “#1 free pool hosting service” and a single create-pool CTA.
  • Features NFL, World Cup, college bracket, Super Bowl squares, bowl pick’ems, and Home Run Derby.
  • Lists public/password-protected pools, custom deadlines, automatic scoring, point spreads, statistics, and logo personalization.
  • Also stresses group email, pool home-page news, message boards, and any-device play.

PoolExpert

poolexpert.com · hockey/baseball specialist

A deeper commissioner product, narrowly focused on hockey and baseball.

  • Calls itself the “ultimate hockey and baseball pool manager experience.”
  • Highlights real-time stat tracking, reports, configuration options, and a mobile app.
  • Promotes draft sessions, box pools, virtual draft tools, and a game builder.
  • Expands into corporate, advertising, fundraising, and office-pool use cases.

Superbru

superbru.com · global adjacent alternative

A polished, mobile-first global game network rather than a commissioner-first host.

  • Leads with “Sports Predictor & Fantasy Games” and a large player-count social-proof claim.
  • Offers Predictor, Fantasy, and Daily Fantasy gameplay across many tournaments.
  • Promotes native apps, premium, and business use cases.
  • Its clean game cards and tournament discovery are a useful quality bar for player-facing UX.

HockeyDraft.ca

hockeydraft.ca · hockey specialist

A Canada-focused hockey manager that sells reliability, support, and familiar formats.

  • Markets a free trial, 15 years of support, and a “best hockey pool manager” claim.
  • Offers standard drafts, box selection pools, keeper leagues, and branded solutions.
  • Promotes playoff pools, cheat sheets, and a cheat-sheet generator.
  • Includes an organization-facing branded solution for media, charities, stores, and leagues.

HostedPools

hostedpools.com · football/hockey/soccer specialist

A formats-first office-pool host for repeat seasonal use.

  • Organizes the pitch by NFL football, NHL hockey, and soccer.
  • Lists loser, margin, survivor, Pick 5, pick’em, 20-point, and playoff formats.
  • Positions itself around management for familiar North American office-pool variants.
  • Its niche is breadth within three sports rather than flexible custom contest design.

AshBracket

ashbracket.com · World Cup-only entrant

An exceptionally clear private-pool story for casual groups.

  • Leads with “Run your own private World Cup pool.”
  • Explains the exact flow: create, invite, make picks, follow standings.
  • Promises private pools, reminders, clear rules, and simple casual-fan participation.
  • Separates organizer and player benefits—an effective homepage structure for PoolWaffle to borrow.

WC26 Predictor

matchpicks.app · World Cup-only entrant

A modern, paid private-pool product that makes the organizer’s job feel lightweight.

  • Hero: “Build your pool. Challenge your friends.”
  • States three modes, a 100-player limit, bonus picks, and “one organizer pays; everyone else just plays.”
  • Offers full tournament, one-country, and knockout-only modes plus top-scorer predictions.
  • Promotes privacy, email invites, trust, transparent pricing, and English/Spanish support.

What this means for PoolWaffle’s homepage

  • Own the commissioner: none of the polished World Cup entrants convincingly sell spreadsheet migration, operational controls, auditability, and custom formats together.
  • Make the first screen tournament-specific. RunYourPool, AshBracket, and WC26 Predictor all convert a generic platform into an immediate World Cup decision.
  • Show the player experience beside the commissioner promise. Modern entrants explain invite → picks → live standings in seconds; legacy hosts mostly list capabilities.
  • Turn “trusted standings” into proof: show a score breakdown, lock status, update freshness, and a public leaderboard preview—not just a feature list.
  • Keep the wide sport/template catalogue below the fold. It establishes range after the hero has answered why a commissioner should launch now.
